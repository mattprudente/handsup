Made for rooms with hybrid meetings

Impossible to miss

The background turns bright lime and gently pulses. Raised hands jump to the top, numbered in the order they went up.

Silent by design

Joins muted, with the camera off and no meeting audio. Add an optional quiet ping, or a silent vibration on iPhone.

Ready before you are

Auto-join on a weekly schedule, or start from a widget or Siri.

Shared rooms

Several Zoom accounts on one iPad, for groups that share a room and host their own meetings.

Readable at a distance

High-contrast colours that work in bright rooms, with Reduce Motion respected.

Pay once

Try the demo with no setup. 14-day free trial, then a one-time unlock. No subscription.

Who it's for

Congregation meetings, churches and faith groups, clubs and societies, councils and committees, classrooms and training rooms. HandsUp works anywhere people join online and raise a hand to speak.

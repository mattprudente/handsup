Privacy policy

Effective 6 November 2026

HandsUp doesn't collect, store or share any personal data with its developer. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Meetings you add: name, meeting ID, display name, colour and schedule.
  • Zoom account details: the account name and Client ID you enter.
  • Secrets: Zoom Client Secrets and meeting passcodes, kept in the device's Keychain and never synced or backed up off the device.
  • Settings: such as ping volume and tiles per page.

This information is shared with HandsUp's widgets on the same device so they can show your meetings. Deleting the app deletes it.

Zoom

To join a meeting, HandsUp uses the Zoom Meeting SDK, which connects to Zoom's service with the credentials and meeting details you entered. The device joins with its microphone and camera off, and HandsUp never plays meeting audio. Participants' names, video and raised hands are shown on screen only. HandsUp doesn't record or store them, or send them anywhere.

Zoom processes meeting data under its own privacy statement. HandsUp asks for camera and microphone permission only because the Zoom SDK requires it to join. HandsUp never turns either on.

Purchases

The free trial, the unlock and optional tips are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details or Apple ID, and receives only anonymous sales totals from Apple.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri or Shortcuts, your meeting names are made available to them on your device so you can say "Join meeting name in HandsUp". This is handled by Apple's own systems.

Children

HandsUp isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
